## Why an assignment, not a license grant

MaiPai keeps a single copyright holder (Jesse Torres) so the project stays
free to dual-license and, if it ever makes sense, sell outright. A
contributor license grant (the GitHub-PR-implies-a-license model) does not
achieve that; an assignment does. Every already-published version stays
AGPL-3.0 forever regardless of who holds the copyright going forward.

## What signing means

By signing this assignment for a specific contribution (a pull request, a
patch, a package), you:

1. Assign to Jesse Torres all right, title, and interest in the copyright
   of that contribution, worldwide, so it can be licensed (including under
   terms other than AGPL-3.0) at his discretion.
2. Confirm the contribution is your own original work, or that you have
   the right to assign it (no copied code you do not hold the rights to,
   no code under an incompatible license).
3. Understand your contribution, once merged, is licensed to everyone
   under the repo's current license (AGPL-3.0), same as the rest of the
   project; the assignment is what lets that license be changed later for
   future versions, not what you personally receive back.
4. Keep the right to be credited by name in the package's `CHANGELOG.md`
   and the catalog's contributor list, unless you ask not to be.
